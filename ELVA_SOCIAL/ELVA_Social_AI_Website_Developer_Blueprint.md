# ELVA Social AI — Website & Developer Blueprint

**Website:** `social.elvatech.in`  
**Product:** ELVA Social AI  
**Initial Platforms:** LinkedIn, Instagram, Facebook  
**Core Model:** AI generation + Human approval + Automated scheduling/publishing

## 1. Product Definition

**ELVA Social AI is an AI-powered social media manager that understands a business, creates its content strategy, generates platform-specific content, takes inspiration from business events and uploaded material, gets human approval, and automatically publishes across LinkedIn, Instagram, and Facebook.**

The product should be positioned as an **AI social media team**, not merely an AI caption generator.

> **Your AI Social Media Team.**

Supporting message:

> Create, review, schedule, and publish social media content automatically — while keeping humans in control.

# 18. AI Agents

Recommended architecture:

- **Brand Understanding Agent** — maintains the brand profile.
- **Content Strategy Agent** — defines and maintains content pillars.
- **Content Planner Agent** — creates the calendar.
- **Topic Generator Agent** — creates candidate topics.
- **Topic Evaluator Agent** — evaluates relevance, originality, usefulness, and repetition.
- **Research / Knowledge Agent** — retrieves approved business knowledge.
- **Content Writer Agent** — writes original content.
- **Platform Adaptation Agent** — creates LinkedIn, Instagram, and Facebook versions.
- **Creative Agent** — generates images and carousel concepts.
- **Review Agent** — checks accuracy, tone, repetition, and platform suitability.
- **Publishing Agent** — handles platform-specific publishing.
- **Analytics Agent** — analyzes performance and feeds learning back into strategy.

# 31. Website FAQ

### What is ELVA Social AI?

ELVA Social AI is an AI-powered social media management platform that creates, reviews, schedules, and publishes content for businesses.

### Which platforms does it support?

The initial version supports LinkedIn, Instagram, and Facebook.

### Does it automatically create content?

Yes. ELVA can generate content based on the business profile, content strategy, knowledge base, previous content, events, and instructions.

### Can I review posts before they are published?

Yes. Human approval is a core part of the workflow.

### Can I edit AI-generated content?

Yes. Users can edit, regenerate, or reject content before publishing.

### Can I upload events?

Yes. Users can tell ELVA about an event and upload photos, videos, PDFs, documents, or other supporting material. ELVA can turn the information into platform-specific content.

### Can I give ELVA examples of content I like?

Yes. ELVA analyzes the structure, tone, and pattern and creates original content for your brand rather than copying the source.

### Will ELVA post the same content on every platform?

No. ELVA is designed to create platform-specific versions of the same underlying idea.

### Can ELVA remember what we posted?

Yes. Publishing history should be used to reduce repetition.

### Can I choose when posts are published?

Yes. Configure schedules, days, times, platforms, and timezone.

### Can ELVA generate images?

Yes. The creative engine can generate supporting visual assets where appropriate.

### Can ELVA create a content calendar?

Yes. ELVA can generate and maintain a content calendar based on your strategy and schedule.

### Does ELVA only create promotional content?

No. The strategy includes educational, informative, industry, thought-leadership, business-problem, automation, and other useful content. Product content is mainly event-driven.

### Can multiple people approve content?

The product should support role-based approval workflows as it evolves.

### Is ELVA only for ELVA Tech?

No. ELVA is the first implementation. The architecture is designed to become a multi-tenant SaaS product for other businesses.
